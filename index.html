📜 HUGO'S ATTIC EXPERIMENT: THE PIGEON-RAT
"I made a pigeon-rat! ... It's practicing for when I sew myself back to Bart!"
— Hugo Simpson, The Simpsons: Treehouse of Horror VII
In "The Thing and I", Bart discovers his evil conjoined twin, Hugo Simpson, locked away in the Simpson attic and sustained on buckets of fish heads fed to him by Homer.

To practice surgical techniques before re-attaching himself to Bart, Hugo crafted the bizarre Pigeon-Rat by sewing the back of a pigeon to the back of a rat!

Now, the unholy creation struggles through Springfield:
  • When the Pigeon takes flight, it tries to escape out the broken attic window, but the heavy rat pulls it down!
  • When the Rat hits the ground, it tries to scurry into sewers and mouse holes, but the flapping pigeon wings get stuck!
